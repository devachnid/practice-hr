# Practice HR: a guide for HR administrators

This guide is for the people who run Practice HR for the practice. That
means setting staff up, keeping their jobs and hours up to date, looking
after leave rules, and producing the monthly payroll file. You are an HR admin when your
login has **Admin status** ticked.

## What you can do

Everything a line manager can do, you can do for anyone. Read the
[guide for line managers](manager.md) for signing in, deciding requests,
recording leave and sickness, balances and the calendar. The differences
for you are:

- **Approvals** shows every request and TOIL claim (a claim for time off in
  lieu; see [TOIL](#toil)) waiting in the practice, not just your team's.
  You can decide any of them except your own. Your own requests go to your
  line manager, or to the other HR admins if you do not have one, and any
  other HR admin can decide them too.
- **Balances**, then **Team balances**, lists everyone, so you can open
  anyone's balances and record leave or TOIL for anyone.
- The **Calendar** shows you the kind of leave too, such as "Away · Study
  leave", where everyone else sees only "Away".
- **Admin** in the menu opens the admin, where everything in the rest of this
  guide is done.

## Going live: your first month

Work through this in order. The rest of this guide explains each step.

1. **Check the contract types and policies.** Under **People**, open
   **Contract types** and check each kind of staff you employ is there, with
   the right full-time week. Under **Absence**, open **Policies**. For
   each contract type, compare the policy with your contracts. Check the days
   a year, when the leave year starts, how bank holidays are handled, and the
   steps for length of service (see [Leave policies in plain words](#leave-policies-in-plain-words)).
   Set **Carry over max days** now if your staff can carry leave into the
   next year: as delivered, nothing carries.
2. **Add your teams** under **People**, **Teams**.
3. **Create a login for each person** (see [How to give someone a login](#how-to-give-someone-a-login)).
   Adding a login sends its invitation straight away and the link lasts
   seven days. If you are not ready for staff to sign in yet, that is fine:
   you can send fresh links later in one go.
4. **Add each person's employee record** and link it to their login.
5. **Add each person's employment**, with a **position** naming their team
   and line manager, and a **contract**. Finish step 4 for everyone first,
   so every manager is there to choose.
6. **Add each person's working pattern.**
7. **Wait for the overnight run.** The next morning, everyone's balances
   for this leave year and next have opened, and bank holidays have been
   charged.
8. **Enter opening balances.** For each person and each kind of leave,
   compare **Remaining** on their **Balances** page with the figure from your
   old system. [Adjust the balance](#adjusting-a-balance) by the difference,
   with a note such as "Opening balance from previous system".
9. **Check the first Balances pages.** Open a few people of different kinds:
   full time, part time, a GP, and someone who started this year. Check the
   **Entitlement**, the bank holiday row and **Remaining** look right.
10. **Tell staff how to sign in.** Under **Access**, **Login accounts**, tick
    everyone and choose **Send invitation or reset link**. Tell them to look
    for an email called "Set up your Practice HR login", and that their
    requests now go to their line manager through Practice HR.

## Finding your way round the admin

Choose **Admin** in the menu. The menu down the left has three groups:

- **People**: Employees, Employments, Teams, Contract types, Audit log.
- **Absence**: Absence types, Policies, Bank holidays, Closed days, Pots,
  Absences, Payroll, Retention.
- **Access**: Login accounts.

A pot is one person's balance of one kind of leave for one leave year.

Three pages are not in that menu: **Working patterns**, **TOIL claims** and
**Email failures**. Find them on the admin home page, the page that opens
when you choose **Admin**. It lists pages by group: **Working patterns** is
under **People**, and the other two are under **Absence**.

The admin home page also shows **Leave requests and TOIL claims waiting**
(anything undecided for more than three working days) and **Emails that did
not go**.

People's records, employments, positions and contracts cannot be deleted. When something changes, you end the old
row and add a new one, so the record always shows what was true on any day.
If a page refuses to save, the message is shown beside the row that is
wrong, and nothing on the page has been saved. Fix that row and save again.

## Setting someone up

A person needs four things: a login, an employee record, an employment
(with a position and a contract), and a working pattern.

### How to give someone a login

1. In the admin, under **Access**, open **Login accounts** and choose **+** (Add login account).
2. Type their **Email** and tick **Admin status** only if they are to be an HR admin.
3. Choose **Save**.

What you will see: their page with a **State** such as "Invited 4 Sep, link
expires 11 Sep". They are emailed a link to choose their own password. You
never see or set anyone's password.

If something goes wrong: if you see "Email isn't set up — copy this link…" or
"Sending to … failed … — copy this link and send it yourself", the email did
not go. Copy the link shown and email it to them yourself. It is shown only
once.

### How to add their employee record

1. Under **People**, open **Employees** and choose **+** (Add employee).
2. Fill in **First name**, **Last name** and **Work email**. Add **Preferred name** if they go by another name; it is shown everywhere instead of their first name.
3. In **User**, choose their login.
4. Add personal details and emergency contacts if you have them, then choose **Save**.

If something goes wrong: a warning that the linked login's email is not the
same as the work email means you should make the two match.

### How to add their employment, position and contract

An employment is one spell of working at the practice. Someone who leaves
and comes back gets a second one.

1. Under **People**, open **Employments** and choose **+** (Add employment).
2. Choose the **Employee** and the **Start date**. Set **Continuous service date** only if their service counts from earlier (for example, from another NHS post). Longer service can give more leave, and it is counted from this date.
3. In the **Positions** section, add a row: **Title**, **Team**, **Line manager**, tick **Primary**, and the **From date**. The line manager of the primary position is the person who decides their requests. Leave **Line manager** blank for someone whose requests should go to the HR admins.
4. In the **Contracts** section, add a row: **Contract type**, **Basis** (Permanent or Fixed term), **From date**, and **Weekly amount** (hours, or sessions for GPs). A fixed-term contract needs a **To date**.
5. Choose **Save**.

### How to add their working pattern

The working pattern says how many hours (or sessions) they work each
morning and afternoon. It decides what a day's leave costs them.

1. On the admin home page, under **People**, open **Working patterns** and choose **+** (Add working pattern).
2. Choose the **Employment** and the **Effective from** date (their start date).
3. Add a row for each day they work: **Weekday** is a number, 0 for Monday up to 6 for Sunday. Give the morning in **Am units** and the afternoon in **Pm units**. Leave out days they do not work.
4. Choose **Save**.

What you will see: if the week does not add up to their contract, a warning
such as "Pattern totals 30 a week; contracts total 37.5." It is saved anyway.
Check whether the pattern or the contract is wrong.

### What happens overnight

Every night, at about half past one, Practice HR does its housekeeping. For
someone new, it:

- opens their balances for this leave year and next, so "Not opened yet: it
  opens overnight." disappears;
- works out their entitlement from their policy, contract and service;
- charges them for the bank holidays they work.

So a new starter's balances appear the morning after you set them up. The
same run closes leave years that have ended, expires old TOIL, switches off
leavers' logins, and emails you about requests that have waited too long.

## Changing someone's hours, pattern or manager

You never edit a position or contract once saved. You end the old one and
add a new one. If you try to edit it, you see "Existing positions and
contracts only end; add a new row for a change."

### How to change someone's hours

1. Under **People**, open **Employments** and open their current employment.
2. In **Contracts**, set the old contract's **To date** to the day before the change.
3. Add a new contract row from the day of the change with the new **Weekly amount**.
4. Choose **Save**.
5. Add a new working pattern with **Effective from** the day of the change (see [How to add their working pattern](#how-to-add-their-working-pattern)).

Their entitlement is recalculated as soon as you save the new contract.

Bank holidays from the change on (but not ones already past) are re-costed overnight. Other leave
already approved keeps the cost it had when it was approved. If that matters,
cancel it (see [Cancelling someone's absence](#cancelling-someones-absence))
and record it again.

### How to change someone's pattern only

Add a new working pattern for their employment with **Effective from** the
day of the change. The old one stays for the days before.

### How to change someone's line manager

1. Open their current employment.
2. In **Positions**, set the old position's **To date** to the day before the change.
3. Add a new position from the day of the change with the new **Line manager**, and tick **Primary**.
4. Choose **Save**.

From that day, their requests go to the new manager, including any already
waiting. The old manager was emailed about those, so tell the new manager.

If something goes wrong: "A person cannot be their own manager." or "That
would make the reporting line a loop." means the manager you chose is wrong.
"There is already a primary position on those dates." means the old
position's **To date** is not set yet.

## When someone leaves

### How to record a leaver

1. Open their current employment.
2. Set **End date** (their last day) and choose a **Leaving reason**.
3. Set the **To date** on their position and contract to the same day.
4. Choose **Save**.

What happens:

- Any leave they had requested or booked that starts after their last day is
  cancelled, and its cost goes back to their balance. They are not emailed.
- The change is written to the **Audit log** (under **People**) with a note
  listing what was cancelled, and anything that could not be. Leave that
  started on or before their last day is left for you to deal with.
- Their entitlement is reduced at once to the part of the year they worked.
- Their login is switched off automatically the night after their last day.
- Their final balance appears on the payroll report's **Leavers** sheet for
  that month. A minus figure is leave taken beyond what they earned; a plus
  figure is leave unused.
- A TOIL claim still waiting can still be decided. After their last day it
  appears only in the HR admins' **Approvals**. Decide it before you run
  their final payroll.

If someone comes back, add a new employment for them, and tick **Active** on
their login account again.

## Leave policies in plain words

A **policy** sets the leave rules for one kind of contract and one kind of
leave. It says how much, when the leave year starts, what carries over, and
how bank holidays are charged. You find them under **Absence**, **Policies**.

**The standard contract** (practice nurses, HCAs, reception,
administration and management staff, who work in hours):

- 22 days a year full time, rising to 23 after one complete year of service,
  25 after three and 27 after five. Part-timers get the same in proportion
  to their hours.
- The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
- Bank holidays are a separate **Bank holiday** balance: one working day for
  each bank holiday in the year, in proportion to their hours.
- Leave is earned in **monthly twelfths** (see below).

**GPs** (partners, salaried GPs and GP trainees, who work in sessions) have
5.6 weeks a year, a leave year from 1 April, and are not charged for bank
holidays.

A **tier** is a step up in leave for length of service, such as "23 days
after 1 year". It is counted from the **Continuous service date** on their
employment. The highest tier reached replaces the lower ones; they do not add
up.

**Monthly twelfths** means a person earns a twelfth of the year's leave for
each month they are employed on any day of it. Part months count as whole
months, for starters and leavers alike.

> **Worked example.** Full time is 37.5 hours a week, so 22 days is 165 hours
> a year. Sam works 22.5 hours a week, so Sam's full year is 165 × 22.5 ÷ 37.5
> = 99 hours. Sam starts on 15 March. March to December is ten months, so
> Sam's first year is 99 × 10 ÷ 12 = 82.5 hours.

**Staff on older terms** (for example, people who joined under TUPE) have
their own contract type, such as "Reception (TUPE 2019)", with its own
policy. Everyone else keeps the standard contract.

### How to change a policy

1. Under **Absence**, open **Policies** and open the policy.
2. Change, for example, **Full-time days per year** or the tiers.
3. Choose **Save**.

What you will see: a message such as "12 pot(s) revised." Everyone affected
has their entitlement updated at once.

A change saved this way applies to the whole leave year. For new terms from a
date, set **Effective to** on the old policy and add a new policy with
**Effective from** the day after.

If something goes wrong: you cannot change when a policy's leave year
starts once people have balances under it. If you try, you see "This type
has leave pots on the current year…". Instead, end the old policy and add a
new one. The new one must start on the first day of its new leave year. That
is the day after the old policy ends. If it does not, adding it shows "This
type has leave pots under another leave year…". Ask for help before doing
this: it is rarely needed.

## Bank holidays and closed days

Bank holidays for England and Wales are already entered up to the end of
2030. Only those count.

### How to add next year's bank holidays

1. Under **Absence**, open **Bank holidays** and choose **+** (Add bank holiday).
2. Enter the **Date**, the **Name**, and "EW" as the **Nation**.
3. Choose **Save**. Repeat for each date, taken from the government's published list.

Do this well before each year starts, from 2031 onwards. A year with no bank
holidays entered charges nothing and gives nobody a bank holiday allowance.
The system warns in its overnight log when fewer than about 400 days of bank
holidays are left. Only whoever looks after the system sees that log, not
you, so put a reminder in your diary.

### How to add a closed day

A closed day is a day the practice is shut that is not a bank holiday. Nobody
is charged for it.

1. Under **Absence**, open **Closed days** and choose **+** (Add closed day).
2. Enter the **Date** and **Reason**, then choose **Save**.

### How to stop charging a bank holiday for one person

Each bank holiday someone works is charged to them automatically, as an
absence of its own. To stop one for one person (for example, because they are
working that day):

1. Under **Absence**, open **Absences**. Filter by **Auto bank holiday** and find their row.
2. Open it and choose **Cancel absence**.
3. On the next page, choose **Cancel absence** again.

The day goes back to their balance and stays cancelled: the overnight run
does not charge it again. Nobody is emailed.

### How to charge a bank holiday again

1. Open the cancelled bank holiday row in **Absences**.
2. Choose **Charge again**, then **Charge again** on the next page.

What you will see: "charged again." The day is charged at once, as a new row.

If something goes wrong: "…nothing was charged" means their working pattern
or policy no longer charges them for that day. For example, they no longer
work that day. Nothing more is needed.

## Adjusting a balance

An adjustment adds to or takes from someone's balance by hand: for example,
to enter an opening balance, give back leave that expired by mistake, or
settle a leaver's balance. Entries are never edited or deleted, so to undo an
adjustment, make another one.

### How to adjust a balance

1. Under **Absence**, open **Pots**. A pot is one person's balance of one kind of leave for one leave year. Filter by the kind of leave, find the row with their name and this leave year's dates, and open it.
2. Choose **Adjust balance**.
3. In **Units**, type the amount: a plus number gives leave back, a minus number takes it away.
4. In **Note**, say why. The person sees this note.
5. Choose **Adjust balance**.

What you will see: "adjusted by +7.50. New balance: …"

If something goes wrong: "This pot is closed." means its leave year has
ended. Adjust the current year's pot instead.

Do not use an adjustment to record TOIL someone has worked: use **Record TOIL
for** on **Team balances**, so the day and reason are kept.

## Cancelling someone's absence

Employees can cancel their own requests at any time, and their own approved
leave until it starts. Line managers cannot cancel anything. You can cancel
anyone else's request or approved absence, at any time, from the admin.

### How to cancel an absence

1. Under **Absence**, open **Absences** and find the row. You can filter by status and type.
2. Open it and choose **Cancel absence**.
3. On the next page, choose **Cancel absence** again.

What you will see: "cancelled." If it was approved, its cost goes back to
their balance. The person's approver is emailed that it was cancelled. The
email reads as though the employee cancelled it, so let them know too.

You cannot cancel your own absence here. Cancel it from your own **Leave**
page, or ask another HR admin if it has already started.

## Family leave and keeping-in-touch days

Maternity, paternity, shared parental and adoption leave are requested like
any leave, with an **Expected start** and **Expected return**.

### How to set the actual start or change the dates

1. Under **Absence**, open **Absences** and open the family leave row.
2. Change **Expected start**, **Actual start** or **Expected return**. These are the only things you can change on an absence.
3. Choose **Save**.

Keeping-in-touch days are added by the person on their own **Leave** page,
one date at a time, within their leave. There is no screen for you to add
them for someone. They appear on the payroll report's **Family leave**
sheet.

## TOIL

TOIL is time off in lieu. It is earned, not given at the start of the year:

- Staff claim it for a day already worked, from **Leave**, then **Claim TOIL**.
  Their line manager decides it. Managers and HR can also record it for
  someone, approved at once.
- Hours staff claim in quarter hours, and GPs in half sessions. There is no
  limit on how much.
- Each amount can be used for twelve months from the day it was worked, then
  any unused part expires. It is used oldest first.
- It is taken by requesting leave of type TOIL.

The twelve months is set on the TOIL absence type, in **Earned expires after
days** (365). Blank means it never expires. A 0 is refused: "Leave blank for
no expiry; 0 would expire it the day it was earned."

### How to see every TOIL claim

On the admin home page, under **Absence**, open **TOIL claims**. It lists
every claim, including decided ones, with who asked, who decided and when.
You cannot change anything there: claims are decided from **Approvals**. An
approved claim cannot be cancelled; correct it with an
[adjustment](#adjusting-a-balance).

## Year end

At the end of each leave year, the overnight run closes every balance
automatically:

- unused leave up to the carry-over limit goes to next year, shown as
  **Carried in**;
- the rest **Expired**;
- a minus balance carries over in full, so it comes off next year;
- unused TOIL carries over in full and keeps its original expiry date.

The carry-over limit is set on each policy, in **Carry over max days** (or
weeks for GPs). Blank means nothing carries. **Carry over expires after
days** can make carried leave expire if it is not booked within that many
days of the new year; blank means never, and 0 is refused.

> **Worked example.** The policy says 5 days carry over. For someone on 37.5
> hours a week that is 5 × 7.5 = 37.5 hours. They have 45 hours left on 31
> December. On 1 January, 37.5 hours is carried in and 7.5 hours expires. For
> someone on 18.75 hours, the limit is 18.75 hours.

**Requests still waiting hold up the close.** A balance is not closed while a
request for that year is still waiting for a decision. It closes the night
after the last one is decided.

### What to check in January

(For GPs, whose leave year starts on 1 April, do the same in April.)

1. Open **Approvals** and decide any request from last year still waiting.
2. Open a few **Balances** pages and check the new year's **Carried in**.
3. Check that this year's and next year's bank holidays are in **Bank holidays**.
4. For leavers with a minus balance, agree with payroll how it is settled, then [adjust](#adjusting-a-balance) their balance to zero with a note.

A balance from a year that has closed cannot be changed. Make any correction
on the current year's balance instead.

## The payroll report

Each month you produce a spreadsheet of changes for the payroll bureau.

### How to generate the month

1. In the admin, under **Absence**, choose **Payroll**. The **Payroll changes** page opens.
2. Under **Generate a month**, type the month in **Month (YYYY-MM)**, for example 2026-09.
3. Choose **Generate and download**.

What you will see: a spreadsheet downloads, named after the month. It is also
listed under **Earlier runs**.

If something goes wrong: "Enter a valid value." means the month was not
typed as year, dash, month, for example 2026-09. "Not a month." means the
month number is not 01 to 12.

The sheets are: **Starters**, **Leavers** (with each leaver's final balance),
**Contract changes**, **Pay changes**, **Sickness** (dates and whether
self-certified, never the kind), **Unpaid**, **Family leave** (with
keeping-in-touch days) and **TOIL**. Leave that runs across a month end is
split between the months. Bank holidays are not listed.

Generating a month again replaces its file with the latest figures, so you
can rerun it after late changes.

## Sickness and confidentiality

- Staff record their own sickness from **Leave**, or their manager records it.
  It needs no approval.
- They choose a **Kind of sickness** in broad terms (Illness, Injury, Mental
  health, Surgery or procedure, Pregnancy-related, Other). Only HR admins can
  see it, on the absence's page in the admin. Opening that page is written
  to the **Audit log**.
- Colleagues and managers see only "Sick" on the calendar.
- Sickness of seven calendar days or fewer is marked self-certified
  automatically.
- National Insurance numbers and pay records are seen only by HR admins, and
  every time someone opens them it is written to the **Audit log**.

## Retention

Under **Absence**, choose **Retention**. It lists people whose records are
past the time the practice keeps them. By default that is six years after
their last employment ended for personal, pay and health records, and seven
years for the audit log. It only lists them. Nothing is deleted automatically, and
there is no delete button: talk to whoever looks after the system about
removing records.

## Logins, passkeys and lockouts

All of these are on the person's page under **Access**, **Login accounts**.

### How to send someone a new password link

1. Open their login account.
2. Choose **Send password-reset link** (or **Send invitation again** if they never set a password).

The link works once, for seven days, and signs them straight in. To send
links to several people, tick them on the list and choose **Send invitation
or reset link**.

### How to help someone who is locked out

After five wrong passwords, password sign-in is paused for an hour. You
cannot lift it, but they can still get in at once with a passkey, or with a
password link: send one as above.

### How to remove a lost passkey

1. Open their login account.
2. Under **Passkeys**, tick the delete box on the lost device's row.
3. Choose **Save**.

They can add a new one from their **Account** page.

### How to make someone an admin of the rota

1. Open their login account.
2. Under **Apps**, tick **Admin of rota**.
3. Choose **Save**.

It takes effect the next time they sign in to the rota. To take it away,
untick the box and save. Anyone with a login can use the rota; this box
only decides who is an admin there.

### How to stop someone signing in

Open their login account, untick **Active**, and choose **Save**. Leavers'
logins are switched off automatically overnight.

## When emails do not go

Practice HR emails approvers, staff and HR. If an email fails, nothing is
lost: the request or decision is still saved. The person is shown a message
with a link to pass on by hand, such as "Saved and waiting for
approval. The email to your approver did not go: send your approver this link
to decide it".

- The admin home page lists the last ten under **Emails that did not go**.
  The full list is under **Email failures**.
- If the admin home page says "Outgoing email is not configured", nobody is
  being emailed. Ask whoever looks after the system, and in the meantime ask
  managers to check **Approvals** each day.
