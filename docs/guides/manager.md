# Practice HR: a guide for line managers

This guide is for anyone who has people reporting to them. You count as a
line manager in Practice HR when HR has named you as someone's line manager.
There is nothing else to switch on: from that day you see **Approvals** and
**My team** in the menu.

## Signing in

### How to sign in

1. Open the Practice HR address your practice gave you. The **Log in** page opens.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Log in**.

Your first login comes from an email called "Set up your Practice HR login".
Open its link, choose a password of at least 12 characters, and choose **Set
password and sign in**. The link works once, for seven days.

### How to add a passkey

A passkey lets you sign in with your face, fingerprint or device PIN instead
of a password. Your password still works.

1. Choose your email address at the top right (on a phone, choose **More**, then your email address). The **Account** page opens.
2. Under **Passkeys**, give it a name such as "my iPhone", type your password if asked, and choose **Add a passkey**.
3. Follow your device's prompt.

Next time, choose **Sign in with a passkey** on the **Log in** page. You are
emailed whenever a passkey is added to your login. If you did not add it,
tell an HR admin.

If something goes wrong: if there is no **Add a passkey** button, this
browser cannot use passkeys; use your password. "Cancelled, or no passkey was
offered." means the device prompt was closed; try again. "This device already
has a passkey here." means you have one already and can use it.

### If you have forgotten your password

1. On the **Log in** page, choose **Forgotten your password?**
2. Type your login email and choose **Send me a link**.
3. Open the email "Reset your Practice HR password", choose a new password and choose **Change password and sign in**.

If something goes wrong: if nothing arrives, check your spam folder, then
ask an HR admin to send you a link.

### If you are locked out

After five wrong passwords you see **Too many attempts**, and signing in with
a password is paused for an hour. You can still get in straight away: choose
**Sign in with a passkey** if you have one on this device, or **Get a password
link by email**. If the wrong passwords were not yours, tell an HR admin.

## Your own leave

Your own details are on **My record**. You book your own leave in the same way as everyone else: choose **Leave**,
then **Request leave**. Your requests go to your own line manager, or to the
HR admins if you do not have one. You never decide your own requests.

## Deciding requests

**Approvals** in the menu shows a number in brackets, such as "Approvals (2)".
That is how many leave requests and TOIL claims (claims for time off in
lieu, explained below) are waiting for you to decide. You are also emailed each one, with a link straight to it.

### How to approve or decline a leave request

1. Choose **Approvals**. Requests are under **Waiting for a decision**.
2. Beside the request, choose **Decide**.
3. Check the details, add a **Comment** if you want to (the employee sees it), then choose **Approve** or **Decline**.

What you will see: **Remaining now** and **Balance after** show what the
person has left before and after this leave. "This is more than their
balance." means they would go below zero; you can still approve it. **Who is
off** lists the rest of their team for each day. If HR has set a minimum for
the team, you see a warning such as "Approving leaves fewer than 3 of
Reception present on 5 Jun 2026."

The employee is emailed "Your Annual leave request was approved" (or
declined), with your comment.

If something goes wrong: if the page says "Already approved" (or declined or
cancelled), someone else has dealt with it, or the employee cancelled it.
Nothing more is needed. If you see "Saved, but the email to … did not go. Let
them know yourself.", your decision is saved: tell the person.

### How to decide a TOIL claim

TOIL is time off in lieu: time someone has worked beyond their hours, which
they can take as leave later.

1. Choose **Approvals**. Claims are under **TOIL claims waiting**.
2. Beside the claim, choose **Decide**.
3. Check the **Day worked**, **Amount** and **Reason**, add a **Comment** if you want to, then choose **Approve** or **Decline**.

Approving adds the time to their TOIL, dated the day they worked it. It can
then be used for a set time from that day, usually twelve months. Once
approved, a claim cannot be undone; if it was a mistake, ask HR. The employee
is emailed your decision, with your comment.

## Recording leave or sickness for your team

Use this when someone phones in sick, or when you have agreed leave with them
in person. It is approved at once, and the person is emailed. Its decision
comment reads "Recorded by" and your name.

### How to record sickness or agreed leave

1. Choose **Leave**. Under **Record leave for someone else**, choose **Record leave for** and the person's name. (You can also get there from **Balances**, then **Team balances**.)
2. Choose the **Type**, the **First day** and the **Last day**. Leave the last day empty for a single day.
3. For a half day, use **On the first day** (Afternoon only) or **On the last day** (Morning only).
4. For sickness, choose the **Kind of sickness** in broad terms, such as Illness or Injury.
5. Choose **Check cost and balance**. Nothing is saved yet.
6. Check the cost, then choose **Confirm and record**.

What you will see: "Sickness for Sam Patel: recorded and approved.", and
their balances page.

Sickness of seven calendar days or fewer (first to last day) is marked as
self-certified automatically. The kind of sickness is seen only by HR: not
by colleagues, and not on any page you see after recording it.

If something goes wrong: "There is already an absence on those dates." means
something is already booked; check their balances. "This crosses the end of
the leave year. Book the two leave years separately." means you need two
entries, one each side of the year end. If you got the dates wrong, ask HR to
cancel it, then record it again.

### How to record TOIL someone has worked

1. Choose **Balances**, then **Team balances**.
2. Beside the person, choose **Record TOIL for** and their name.
3. Fill in **Day worked**, **Hours worked in lieu** (in quarter hours: 1.25 is an hour and a quarter; for a GP, **Sessions worked in lieu**, in half sessions), and **What for**.
4. Choose **Record and approve**.

What you will see: "TOIL for Sam Patel: recorded and approved." They are
emailed.

If something goes wrong: "A claim is for time already worked: the day cannot
be after today." means the day is in the future. A message ending "so it
would already have expired" means the day is too long ago to claim.

## Your team's balances

### How to check someone's balance

1. Choose **Balances**, then **Team balances**.
2. Beside the person, choose **Balances**.

What you will see: a row for each kind of leave, for this leave year and the
next. Choose any figure to see the entries behind it (the "ledger"), or
choose **Ledger** for all of them.

If something goes wrong: "Not opened yet: it opens overnight." is normal for
someone new: the balance appears the next morning. "… has no current
employment, so there are no balances to show." means they have left or not
started yet; ask HR.

Each row is a pot: one person's allowance of one kind of leave for one leave
year. Their pot is their balance for that year. The words on that page mean:

- **Leave year**: the twelve months a balance covers. For most staff it runs
  from 1 January; for GPs it may start on 1 April.
- **Entitlement**: what they earn this leave year.
- **Carried in**: leave brought forward from last year.
- **Taken**: leave that has already happened.
- **Booked**: approved leave still to come.
- **Waiting**: requests nobody has decided yet. These do not count until approved.
- **Expired**: leave they lost because it was not used in time.
- **Adjustments**: changes HR has made by hand, and TOIL earned.
- **Remaining**: what is left. It shows in red if it is below zero.

**My team** in the menu lists the people who report to you, with their
position, contracted hours or sessions, and the hours in their weekly
pattern. You cannot change anything there.

## The calendar

### How to see who is off

1. Choose **Calendar**. It opens on this month, for the whole practice.
2. To see one team, choose it under **Team**, then choose **Show**.
3. To change month, choose **Previous** or **Next**.

What you will see: each person who is off, by day. With a team chosen, each
day also shows how many are in, for example "4 of 6".

Only approved absences appear. Everyone, you included, sees each person's
name and a plain label such as "Leave", "Sick" or "Away", never the reason.

## Reminders when a request waits

Sometimes a request or TOIL claim is not decided within three working days.
Weekends, bank holidays and practice closures do not count. When that
happens, the HR admins are emailed about it once, and it is listed on their
admin home page. You are
not sent a second email, so check **Approvals** regularly. HR may chase you,
or decide it themselves.

## What you cannot do, and who to ask

Ask an HR admin to:

- cancel an absence for someone in your team (the employee can cancel their
  own request at any time, and their own approved leave until it starts);
- correct a balance or undo an approved TOIL claim;
- change someone's hours, working pattern, team or line manager;
- set up a new starter or record a leaver;
- send a new login link to someone who cannot sign in.

Keeping-in-touch days during maternity, paternity, shared parental or
adoption leave are added by the employee on their own **Leave** page.
You cannot add them.
